## Brief Description (525 of 600 characters)

Trust Issues protects UAE families from scam links, messages, phone numbers and files. A guardian links parents' and children's phones with one QR code: scam sites are blocked in every app, risky ones wait for the guardian's OK with our verdict shown, and SOS reaches the family. Our own model, trained on 589,687 real URLs, with a sandbox browser and two AI judges, caught 99.3% of live scam sites with zero false alarms on 411 UAE bank and government sites. Police get a live radar, cases, scam networks and evidence packs.

## Tools Used

Python, FastAPI, PostgreSQL, React, TypeScript, Tailwind CSS, Playwright, LightGBM, scikit-learn, Anthropic Claude API, OpenAI API, VirusTotal, URLhaus, SerpAPI, Google libphonenumber, DNS-over-HTTPS, Web Push, Docker, Render
