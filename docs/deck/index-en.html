02The gap

Scanners need a suspicious victim. The targets are not.

The family's problem

  • The most targeted are the least suspicious: parents and elders, children, household helpers.
  • None of them will open an app to check a link that arrived by WhatsApp or SMS.
  • The message looks official: a traffic fine, Salik, UAE PASS, or "your bank account is suspended".
  • Nobody in the family knows until the money has been paid.

The police's problem

  • The report arrives after the harm, and recovery is hard.
  • A fake site lives for hours, then moves to a new name.
  • Each site is part of a network: the same server, the same template, the same phone number.
  • Collecting evidence and asking for a takedown, site by site, costs precious time.

How we trained it

  • LightGBM on 73 features of the link, stacked with a character n-gram model and calibrated.
  • An honest test (time-split): scam links first seen after the training date, and no domain shared between training and test.
  • Tested on 33,519 more URLs.
  • Runs on our own server: 0.26 ms per link, with no outside service.
  • A second, domain-only model powers phone protection through DNS.
